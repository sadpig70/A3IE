# STEP 5 — Top-3 Idea Selection Prompt

Select the 3 most valuable ideas among all ideas generated in STEP 4.

## Selection Criteria
1. Feasibility — technological realizability
2. Impact — market or industry influence
3. Integrity — structural and logical consistency
4. Novelty — originality and creativity

## Output Requirements
For each selected idea:
- Provide selection rationale
- Provide risk/benefit summary
